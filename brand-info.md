## What we sell

Ready-to-wear eyewear only — **no prescription fitting and no lens grinding**:

- **Sunglasses** — UV400, categories 2 and 3, some polarised
- **Optical frames** — sold on their own with demo lenses; the customer's optician fits the lenses
- **Blue-light glasses** — non-prescription screen filters from 40% to 90%
- **Reading glasses** — ready-made, +1.00 to +3.50, same strength in both eyes
- **Kids** — flexible frames and UV400 kids' sunglasses
- **Accessories** — cases, cords, cleaning kit

## Positioning

Real UV protection, honest build quality, and a price that makes sense. The store
competes on being trustworthy about what the lenses actually do, not on being cheapest.
